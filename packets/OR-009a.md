# OR-009a — Digest news rule and canonical example

```
TASK: OR-009a
BRANCH: fix/digest-news-rule

OBJECTIVE
The digest sends only when it has something new to say, and one
consistent example is used everywhere.

WHY
A note that is only the owner's own deed and loan tells them nothing
they already know, and would be identical every month. That is how
complaint rate climbs. The fixture, the sample, and marketing also
drifted onto different numbers for the same house.

SCOPE
- Skip rule in the digest renderer
- Fixture scenarios, including the full Oakdale example
- A test that the tax line is "about $2,600"
- Marketing page and /sample use the same numbers
- Out of scope: send history / "new since last send" (S4),
  tax formula changes, schema, parcel map, sending

DESIRED BEHAVIOR
1. News rule. Replace the two-block skip rule:
   Send only if at least one NEWS block renders:
     - street sales
     - four doors down
     - property taxes
     - a document recorded on their own parcel within 45 days of asOf
       (a paid-off or refinanced loan is news)
   The record block and the loan block are context and never count as
   news on their own.
   Skip reason: "Nothing new on their street this month."
2. Canonical example — update the `full` scenario to exactly:
     1142 Oakdale Ave, La Verne 91750 · owner Marilyn Okafor
     Grant deed recorded 2019-03-14 · doc 2019-0248117 · $712,000
     Assessed value $817,800
     Recorded street sales, trailing 12 months:
       1108 Oakdale Ave  $1,120,000  recorded May 1
       1162 Oakdale Ave  $1,040,000  recorded Jul 22
       2334 Bonita Ave     $985,000  recorded Jun 9
     Street median: $1,040,000
     Four doors down (MLS, active): 1187 Oakdale Ave, $1,065,000,
       listed Aug 12 — NOT among the recorded sales
     Deed of trust $569,600 · Cardinal Home Loans · 2019-03-14
   Tax benefit = (1,040,000 − 817,800) × CA_TAX rate → "about $2,600"
3. A test asserting that exact computation.
4. Add scenarios: "static only" (record + loan, nothing else → skip)
   and "recent payoff" (record + loan + a reconveyance recorded
   20 days ago → send).
5. Report what produced $7,100 in the previous fixture.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
1. The no-street-sales scenario now skips
2. "Recent payoff" sends; "static only" skips
3. The full scenario produces "about $2,600", proven by test
4. 1187 Oakdale appears only as the listing, never as a recorded sale
5. The $7,100 cause is reported
6. pnpm verify passes, CI green. Do NOT merge.

DO NOT
- Change the tax formula unless item 5 finds it wrong
- Count the record or loan block as news
- Read a database inside renderDigest
- Add a dependency
- Change the schema, ca-tax.ts, PROJECT_STATE.md, CLAUDE.md, BUILD_PLAN.md
- Display a loan payoff balance
- Assert Prop 19 eligibility
```
