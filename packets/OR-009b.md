# OR-009b — Own-parcel recordings are not news

```
TASK: OR-009b
BRANCH: fix/digest-news-rule (existing PR #15; do not open a new PR)

OBJECTIVE
The "recent payoff" email is the problem — its only news is telling
Marilyn about her own refinance, which reads as her agent watching
her mortgage. Remove her own recordings from the news set, so that
scenario skips unless her street has activity.

WHY
A note whose only news is a document on their own house tells them
something they already did. That is how a monthly refinance notice
starts to feel like surveillance.

SCOPE
- News rule in the digest renderer
- Fixture scenarios and news tests
- Out of scope: tax formula, schema, send history, a second PR

DESIRED BEHAVIOR
1. News that can trigger a send is ONLY:
   - street sales
   - four doors down
   - property taxes
2. A document recorded on their own parcel (payoff, refinance,
   reconveyance, grant deed, anything) is NOT news. The record
   block and the loan block remain context and never count as news.
3. The existing "recent payoff" scenario (record + loan + a
   reconveyance recorded ~20 days ago, no street activity) MUST
   skip. Skip reason stays: "Nothing new on their street this month."
4. If a recent own-parcel recording is present AND there is street
   news (sales / four doors / taxes), the email still sends and may
   still mention the payoff/refinance as context — it just cannot
   be the sole reason to send.
5. Add or adjust a scenario that proves: recent payoff + street
   activity → send; recent payoff alone → skip.
6. Update tests that asserted own-parcel-within-45-days was news.
   The purity test and tax ~$2,600 test must still pass. Canonical
   Oakdale numbers must not drift.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
1. News is only street sales, four doors, and property taxes
2. "Recent payoff" alone skips with the street-nothing reason
3. Recent payoff + street activity still sends; payoff may appear
   as loan context
4. Purity test and about $2,600 still pass; Oakdale numbers hold
5. pnpm verify passes, CI green on PR #15, then merge

DO NOT
- Merge until OR-009b is committed, pushed to PR #15, and CI is green
- Change the tax formula or src/config/ca-tax.ts
- Count record or loan as news
- Add a dependency
- Change schema, PROJECT_STATE.md, CLAUDE.md, BUILD_PLAN.md
- Display a loan payoff balance
- Assert Prop 19 eligibility
- Files over 300 lines
- Invent a second PR
```
